元素战争 · 蒙德城Element War · Mondstadt

把城市探索、元素反应与射击战斗放进同一个浏览器世界。City exploration, elemental reactions and shooting combat in one browser world.

JavaScript · Three.js · WebGL · GLB

把元素反应带进射击游戏Elemental reactions meet a shooter

  • 四名可选角色，七种元素武器；支持第一人称与肩后视角切换。Four playable characters and seven elemental weapons, with first-person and over-the-shoulder views.
  • 通过冻结、蒸发等反应组合攻击；每种武器独立管理弹匣与射击参数。Combine attacks with reactions such as Freeze and Vaporize; each weapon has its own magazine and firing parameters.
  • 在蒙德城移动、跳跃与攀爬，迎战敌人波次、巡弋无人机和首领。Move, jump and climb through Mondstadt while facing enemy waves, patrol drones and bosses.

展示画面来自已有游戏实机记录；2026-11-15 完整版包含游戏源码、模型、音效与本地启动器。Images come from existing in-game captures. The 15 November 2026 release includes source code, models, audio and a local launcher.

超市收银系统Supermarket Checkout

从选购到收款，完成一段可验证的结账流程。A verifiable checkout flow, from product selection to payment.

  • 五种预设商品、购物车与库存检查Five preset products, cart and stock checks
  • 数量与商品编号检查、空购物车提示Quantity / product ID checks and empty-cart feedback
  • 金额汇总、付款重试、找零与收据Totals, payment retries, change and receipts

实际运行：Water × 2 + Milk × 1，总额 13；支付 20，找零 7，钱箱余额 3013。Actual run: Water × 2 + Milk × 1 totals 13; payment 20, change 7, final cashbox 3013.

  • Student 类封装姓名、测验与实验成绩Student class encapsulates name, test and lab marks
  • 总分 = 测验 70% + 实验 30%Total = 70% test + 30% lab
  • A ≥ 80、B ≥ 70、C ≥ 60、D ≥ 50，其余 FA ≥ 80, B ≥ 70, C ≥ 60, D ≥ 50; otherwise F

四位预设学生实际输出等级为 B、C、D、A。The four preset students produce grades B, C, D and A.
